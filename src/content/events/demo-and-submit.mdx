Welcome to the final Budapest session, whether you shipped the original idea, a much smaller version, or a useful lesson wrapped in a working demo. The room is here to help you present the result clearly and close the last open loops.

## Goals

- Practice a concise demo that shows the value of the finished work.
- Catch missing submission details while other builders are available to help.
- Press submit with confidence and celebrate the progress made across the series.

## What to bring

Bring the build you can demonstrate, your draft submission, and any public links or media it needs. Keep passwords, signing keys, and other credentials private while reviewing the final checklist.

The exact time and Budapest venue will be published once confirmed.
