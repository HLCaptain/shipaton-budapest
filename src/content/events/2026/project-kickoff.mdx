🚀 Join the **first-ever Shipaton IRL event organized in Budapest** and turn your app idea—or early curiosity—into a real project.

**Primary languages:** English and Hungarian.

🍕 **Food and drinks will be provided at the venue.**

Project Kickoff combines lightning talks from local mobile and technology experts, hands-on support from experienced mentors, and interactive workshops for brainstorming, meeting builders, and starting projects.

**You do not need an existing idea or team. Solo builders are welcome.**

## Lightning talks

🎤 Three focused **15-minute talks** will cover:

- Fast mobile project initialization and shipping
- Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform
- AI-assisted coding: cognitive surrender and comprehension debt

## Interactive workshops

🎮 Short games and audience activities will help you:

- Meet people with similar interests and complementary skills
- Explore, brainstorm, and refine app ideas
- Find potential teammates, feedback partners, or projects to join

Joining a team is optional. You can also work independently and use the session to meet mentors and other builders.

## Mentoring and open workshop

🧭 During the main workshop, **4–5 mentors** will circulate between attendees and help with:

- Turning an idea into a realistic first version
- Project setup, technical direction, and AI-assisted development
- Product, UX/UI, mobile engineering, and monetization questions

The lightning-talk speakers will also join the workshop for follow-up questions and discussions.

Come with an app idea, a half-formed concept, or simply the motivation to build something. The goal is to leave with **a clearer direction, useful connections, and a concrete starting point for Shipaton.**
