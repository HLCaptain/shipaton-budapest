Start your Shipaton project with an idea, a team, a technical direction, and access to experienced mentors.

**Primary language:** English.

Project Kickoff is a hands-on evening for mobile developers, designers, product people, indie builders, and anyone interested in building mobile apps with AI.

The event combines three focused lightning talks with interactive team-building, brainstorming, and an open project workshop. You can attend with an existing app idea, a half-formed concept, or no project at all.

This is not an all-night hackathon. The goal is to help you get moving and leave with a realistic next step.

## What you can expect

### Lightning talks

Three speakers will each deliver a focused 15-minute talk covering:

- Fast mobile project initialization and shipping
- Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform
- AI-assisted coding, cognitive surrender, and comprehension debt

The speakers will remain available during the workshop for questions and mentoring.

### Interactive team building

Short audience activities and live polls will help you:

- Meet people with similar interests
- Find potential teammates or feedback partners
- Discover projects you may want to join
- Identify complementary skills in the room
- Explore possible Shipaton app ideas

You do not need to join a team. Solo builders are welcome and can use the activities to find mentors, testers, and accountability partners.

### Open workshop and mentoring

After the talks and interactive activities, the room will turn into an open project workshop. You can use the session to:

- Brainstorm and validate an app idea
- Reduce an idea to a realistic first version
- Find or form a team
- Initialize a mobile project
- Choose a technical direction
- Sketch an onboarding or core user flow
- Plan how to use AI during development
- Review AI-generated code and technical decisions
- Discuss Kotlin Multiplatform or Compose Multiplatform
- Explore monetization and RevenueCat integration
- Get product, UX/UI, and engineering feedback

A group of experienced mentors will circulate throughout the workshop and help attendees directly with their projects.

## Who should attend?

- Android and iOS developers
- Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform developers
- Flutter and React Native developers
- Backend and AI engineers
- UX/UI designers
- Product managers
- Indie developers and solo builders
- Students and early-career developers
- People looking for a Shipaton team or project
- People who are curious about Shipaton but have not committed yet

All experience levels are welcome.

## What should you bring?

- A laptop and charger if you plan to join the workshop
- An app idea, project, or problem you would like to explore
- Any sketches, repositories, prototypes, or notes you already have
- A willingness to share unfinished ideas and help other builders

An existing idea, team, or project is not required.

## Registration

Registration is required because the event is hosted at the Genesys Hungary office.

After registering, you may optionally complete a short questionnaire about your skills, interests, project ideas, and mentoring needs. The answers will help us prepare the workshop and suggest useful connections during the event.

Final venue-entry details will be shared with registered attendees.

## About Shipaton Budapest

Shipaton Budapest is a local event series for developers and product builders participating in Shipaton 2026.

The series focuses on practical progress: finding ideas, forming teams, starting projects, getting feedback, integrating monetization, finding early users, and ultimately shipping a real mobile app.
