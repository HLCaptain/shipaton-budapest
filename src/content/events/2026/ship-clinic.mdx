Welcome to a practical problem-solving room for projects nearing the finish line. Bring the flow, decision, or release concern that is costing you momentum, and we will work through it with direct, respectful feedback.

## Goals

- Find the highest-impact issue between the current build and a confident release.
- Test onboarding, purchase, or core product flows with fresh eyes.
- Turn store-readiness concerns into a short, ordered cut list.

## What to bring

Bring a testable build if you have one, plus the device and accounts needed to demonstrate it safely. You control what you share; a screenshot, prototype, or written flow is enough when code or data must remain private.
