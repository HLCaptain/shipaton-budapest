🎉 Join **Shipaton Budapest** and **Kotlin Budapest** for a joint celebration of **Kotlin’s 15th birthday** and the wrap-up of **Shipaton 2026**, RevenueCat’s app-building competition. One meetup, two communities, and plenty to talk about.

**Event language: English.** Times are local to Budapest (Europe/Budapest).

## Shipaton wrap-up

We’ll start with the Shipaton Budapest team’s look back at the competition: who took part, what they built, and the lessons learned along the way.

## Kotlin Open Space

💬 There are no fixed discussion topics or scheduled speakers for this part: anyone can suggest a topic, ask a question, or share a personal Kotlin story on a sticky note. The room will form smaller groups around whatever gets people talking.

Come with a question, a war story, or just curiosity—**no preparation is needed**. We’ll round off the evening with networking, a bit of trivia, and time to catch up.

## Venue and accessibility

**Genesys Hungary — Eiffel Office Building**  
Teréz körút 55–57, 1062 Budapest  
**Building B, 6th floor. Entrance next to Café Frei.**

The venue is accessible. If you need sign language interpretation or live text transcription, or would like us to reserve a front-row seat, please contact the organizers in advance through the [Meetup event page](https://www.meetup.com/kotlin-budapest/events/316663708/). We will do our best to accommodate your needs.

**Event sponsor:** Genesys Hungary.
