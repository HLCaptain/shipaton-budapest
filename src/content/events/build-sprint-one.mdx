Welcome whether your project is a blank repository, a rough prototype, or a build that has stopped moving. This is a working session: the useful outcome is progress you can test, not a polished presentation.

## Goals

- Finish one small, testable slice of the product.
- Get practical help with a blocker instead of carrying it alone.
- Leave with a clear next milestone and less uncertainty than you arrived with.

## What to bring

Bring your laptop, project access, and the device or browser you use for testing. A short note describing your current blocker will make it easier for someone else to help quickly.
