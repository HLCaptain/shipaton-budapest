Welcome to the Budapest starting line. Come with an idea, a half-formed problem, or simply the wish to build alongside other people; a finished concept and a team are not prerequisites.

## Goals

- Meet local builders and find collaborators if that helps your project.
- Cut an ambitious idea down to a version that can genuinely ship.
- Define a first milestone that is small enough to start immediately.

## What to bring

Bring a laptop or notebook and any rough ideas you already have. If you are undecided, the workshop will help you choose a useful problem and a realistic first step.

The exact time and Budapest venue will be published once confirmed.
